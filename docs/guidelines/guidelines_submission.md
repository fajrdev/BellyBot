Guidelines for Conceptual Design Submission and Judging Criteria

A.  Additional Details of Problem Statement

Confined working envelope < 1.2-meter height (Narrow Body (NB) aircraft Cargo Hold space), Irregular baggage
geometry, material and fragility, 20–32 kg, High throughput target ≥ 12 bags/minute, floor load limits ≤ 600
kg/m².

B.  Conceptual Design Documents and the Formats for Submission

1.  Design diagrams/drawings/3D CAD Model (STEP/STP): Overall concept, major functional elements
2.  Animation / Video (Max 2 minutes) -MP4: Demonstrate how the proposed system operates.
3.  Report of design concept (5–8 pages) -PDF: Concept Overview, System Architecture, Mechanism / Key
Design Details, Loading and Unloading Operations, Safety, Advantages and Limitations with necessary
pictures/diagram etc. Can add more design solutions pictures (different views etc) as Annex if required, at
end of the report. Add a cover page with Team name, Lead and Members name and email/contact details,
Institute etc.
•  An optional Presentation PPT file also can be added with design pictures and annotations.

C.  Judging Criteria

Innovation & Creativity (20%), Functional Effectiveness & Baggage Handling Strategy (20%), Feasibility & Technical
Soundness  (15%),  NB  Aircraft  Cargo-Hold  Compatibility  &  Space  Utilisation  (15%),  Autonomy  &  Intelligent
Operation (15%), Safety, and Human Interaction (15%)
